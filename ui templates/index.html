How the Two Interfaces Govern Multi-Tenancy

1

Commercial Quota Allocation

Super Admins allocate seat quotas per institution. When Zenith Bank consumes 8 of its 10 seats, the system triggers the 80% threshold warning in the Tenant Admin UI.

2

Self-Service Governance

Tenant Admins manage their own engineers and viewers, assign RBAC permissions, and submit capacity expansion requests directly to Platform Operations.

3

Supervised Support Access

When an engineer needs help with an ISO 20022 message, Support requests time-limited impersonation with an immutable audit log trail visible to both parties.
